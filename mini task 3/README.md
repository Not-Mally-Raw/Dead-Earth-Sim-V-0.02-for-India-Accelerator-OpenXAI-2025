Netflix Movies and TV Shows Dataset EDA

This project explores the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset from Kaggle using Seaborn and Matplotlib.

Dataset:
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows
Description: Details of movies and TV shows available on Netflix, including genre, cast, release year, ratings, and more.

Visualizations:
- Movies vs TV Shows
- Top Countries
- Release Year Boxplot
- Top Genres
- Movie Duration Swarm

Insights:
- Netflix has more movies than TV shows.
- The US, India, and the UK produce the most content.
- Most content is from recent years.
- Drama, International Movies, and Comedy are the most common genres.
- Most movies are between 80-120 minutes.
